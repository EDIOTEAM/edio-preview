About EDIOKarur, Tamil Nadu

We started close to the repair bench.

EDIO didn't begin with a market study. It began with boards on a bench, and with technicians who could see exactly what was wrong with a device but not always get to it.

01 Origin

The hard part is rarely the soldering. It's knowing what to trust.

Spend time there and one thing becomes obvious: the hard part is rarely the soldering. It's knowing what to trust. Which measurement matters. Whether the board is dead or just holding the wrong bytes.

That's a technology problem, and nobody had treated it like one. So we started building tools for it — in something that works standalone, in the real conditions of a workshop.

SmartClone came out of that. Then a second realisation: every repair it helps finish produces knowledge, and almost all of it evaporates. Structuring that knowledge is where EDIO is heading.
